Privacy policy

Grandma's Little Pet

This is the privacy policy of the game Grandma's Little Pet (Bichinho da Vovó), made by Vitor Delgallo, an independent developer in Brazil.

Summary

The game does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no sign-up, ads, purchases, chat between players, or analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on the device

Progress — the pet's name, the saved game's name, coins, the garden, achievements, settings and the play time used by parental supervision — is stored only on the device itself. We have no access to any of it.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, Android itself may keep a copy of these files in the person's Google account, to restore them when the game is reinstalled or the phone is replaced. This is done by Google, under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive that copy. Backup can be turned off in the Android settings.

Internet and links

The game works without internet. The only links are the buttons to follow profiles on Instagram, which open — outside the game — only after an adult answers a question, and which disappear when parental supervision is on. What happens on Instagram follows its own rules and privacy policy.

Permissions

The game only uses the permission to vibrate the device, for the vibration on touch, which can be turned off in the game's Settings.

Children and teenagers

The game is for all ages, including children, and was made to respect Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law 15.211/2025) and General Data Protection Law (Law 13.709/2018). Age rating: suitable for all ages. It collects no one's data, has no loot boxes or purchases, and offers, in Settings → For parents and guardians, the play time of each day and a supervision an adult can turn on: a daily time limit, with links that leave the game hidden. On Android, Google's Family Link also helps follow and limit use.

Deleting the data

To delete everything, just uninstall the game or clear its data in the Android settings. Inside the game, "Delete" on a saved game only removes it from the list: it can be recovered later.

Contact and reports

Questions about privacy, requests, and reports of any violation of children's and teenagers' rights related to the game: vitor.delgallo@outlook.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and inside the game.
